# Sales-Dashboard

Project Description

Developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales data, monitor business performance, and generate meaningful insights. The dashboard provides a clear view of revenue, profitability, sales trends, product performance, regional performance, and sales representative productivity to support data-driven decision-making.

Project Objectives

Monitor key sales performance indicators and business growth.

Analyze sales and profit trends across monthly and yearly periods.

Identify high-performing and low-performing products and categories.

Evaluate regional sales performance and identify business opportunities.

Compare sales representative performance based on revenue and profit.

Analyze order volumes, product quantities, and sales distribution.

Provide interactive reports for effective business monitoring.

Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and data visualization.

Power Query – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.

DAX – Calculated measures and business KPI calculations.

Microsoft Excel – Source data management and validation.

Key Features

KPI Monitoring: Displays Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Total Quantity.

Sales Trend Analysis: Tracks monthly and yearly sales and profit performance.

Product Analysis: Identifies top-selling products and compares category performance.

Regional Analysis: Evaluates sales contribution across regions, states, and cities.

Sales Representative Analysis: Compares individual sales performance and profitability.

Interactive Filtering: Uses slicers to filter reports by date, region, category, and sales channel.

Detailed Analysis: Uses drill-through to explore product-level and sales-level details.

Dynamic Reporting: Updates visuals based on selected filters for faster analysis.

Data Preparation & Transformation

Imported sales data from Microsoft Excel into Power BI.

Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query.

Corrected data types and standardized date and numeric fields.

Checked data quality, missing values, and duplicate records.

Created Year, Month, and Day fields for time-based analysis.

Prepared the dataset for accurate reporting and visualization.

DAX & KPI Analysis

Created DAX measures to calculate and analyze:

Total Sales and Total Profit.

Total Orders and Total Quantity.

Average Order Value.

Profit Margin Percentage.

Monthly and yearly sales performance.

Business Insights

The dashboard helps business users:

Identify sales growth patterns and monthly performance changes.

Understand which products and categories contribute most to revenue.

Compare regional sales contributions and profitability.

Evaluate sales representative performance.

Monitor profit margins and overall business performance.

Make informed decisions using interactive reports and visual insights.

Project Outcome

Successfully developed an interactive business intelligence dashboard that converts raw sales data into meaningful performance indicators and actionable insights. The project demonstrates practical skills in data preparation, DAX calculations, data visualization, and business reporting using Power BI.
